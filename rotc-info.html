ROTC Program Overview

World Class • Multi-Mission Ready • Cross-Domain Capable

Elite Military Leadership Development

The Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) is a comprehensive program designed to develop future military leaders through rigorous training, character building, and academic excellence. Our program combines traditional military values with modern leadership techniques.

Core Values

  • Honor and Integrity
  • Patriotism and Service
  • Duty and Responsibility
  • Excellence in All Things

Training Focus

  • Leadership Development
  • Military Tactics
  • Physical Fitness
  • Character Building

Training Program Phases

Progressive Development Journey
1

Foundation Phase

Introduction to military fundamentals, basic drill and ceremony, physical fitness foundation, and core military values. Students learn the basics of military structure and hierarchy.

2

Development Phase

Advanced military tactics, leadership principles, team building exercises, and specialized training modules. Focus on developing critical thinking and decision-making skills.

3

Leadership Phase

Command responsibilities, strategic planning, advanced physical training, and mentoring junior cadets. Preparation for officer roles and responsibilities.

4

Commission Phase

Final assessments, commissioning preparation, career planning, and transition to active duty or reserve status. Graduation and officer commission ceremony.
